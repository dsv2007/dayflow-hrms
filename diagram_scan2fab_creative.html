Idea: Scan2Fab AI

The NeRF Reverse Engineering Workflow

Process Speed
10 SECONDS
Step 01

Orbital Capture

An unskilled factory worker uses any standard smartphone to record a quick 10-second orbital video of the broken physical part.

Zero Hardware Cost
Step 02

NeRF Cloud AI

The video frames are processed by Frontier Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) and Gaussian Splatting to extrapolate perfect 3D depth and geometry.

Generative Spatial AI
Step 03

Digital Mesh Synthesis

The neural point cloud is instantly converted into a watertight, mathematically perfect polygonal mesh and exported as a CAD file.

.STEP / .STL Format
Step 04

Factory Execution

The perfectly synthesized digital twin is sent directly to existing CNC machines or industrial 3D printers for instant fabrication.

Immediate Production
