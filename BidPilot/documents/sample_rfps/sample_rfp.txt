# REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL: Department of Education Laptop Procurement

**RFP ID:** DOE-2026-0817
**Deadline:** September 15, 2026

## 1. Project Overview
The Department of Education is seeking a vendor to supply, configure, and deliver 500 laptops for the upcoming school year.

## 2. Vendor Requirements
Vendors submitting a proposal must meet the following criteria:
1. **Experience:** Must have at least 3 years of experience operating in the IT procurement space.
2. **Delivery Timeline:** Must be able to deliver all 500 laptops within 60 days of contract award.
3. **Security:** Vendor must hold an active ISO 27001 certification.
4. **Project Management:** The vendor must assign a dedicated Project Manager with a PMP certification.

## 3. Proposal Submission
Proposals must include:
- A brief company history demonstrating required experience.
- A proposed delivery schedule.
- Evidence of required certifications.
- Resumes of key personnel.
- Pricing table.
